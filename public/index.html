Free for families

Share the driving to your kids' activities

Carpooler works out who drives when, fairly, for practices, lessons and anything else your families share rides to.

How it works

  1. Create a carpoolEnter the activity, where it is, and which days and times it happens.
  2. Invite the other familiesShare the carpool's 6-character code. Each family adds their riders, pickup addresses and the days they can drive.
  3. Get a fair scheduleCarpooler takes turns between drivers for every drop-off and pick-up, and shows each driver their riders and route.

Your information stays in your carpool

You'll sign in with Google or your email address when you create or join a carpool. We use your name and email address to identify you and send you carpool updates. Addresses and riders' names are shown only to members of your carpool, and we never sell your information or use it for ads. See our Privacy Policy.
